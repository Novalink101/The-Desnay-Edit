Our Policies

Orders & Confirmation

A 50% deposit is required to confirm your booking and allow design work to begin. Please provide all wording, names, dates and other details accurately.

Payment

The remaining 50% balance is payable once your design is complete and before collection of the printed newspaper. Your order can only be released once payment has been received in full.

Printing & Colour

Printed newspapers are available in A4 and A3. Black and white is the standard finish; colour printing is available at an additional cost.

Design Approval & Changes

Please review your design carefully and check all names, dates, spelling and wording. Any requested changes must be made before the design is sent for printing. Changes after printing has started may not be possible.

Cancellations & Refunds

The 50% deposit covers the booking and design work. Once design work has started, the deposit is non-refundable. If you need to cancel or discuss a concern, please contact me as soon as possible.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What occasions do you design for?

A: Birthdays, matric dances, graduations, anniversaries, surprises and many other special occasions.

Q: What sizes are available?

A: Printed newspapers are available in A4 and A3.

Q: How long does an order take?

A: Please allow 2-3 days for completion once all required details have been received.

Q: Do you offer delivery?

A: Delivery is not currently available. Collection arrangements will be confirmed when your order is ready.

Q: When can I request changes?

A: Please request changes and check all details before the design goes to print.

Q: How does payment work?

A: A 50% deposit is required before design work begins. The remaining 50% is due before collection.
